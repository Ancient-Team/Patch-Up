# Patch Up

![Minecraft](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft-1.21.1-green)
![Loader](https://img.shields.io/badge/Loader-Fabric-beige)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Apache--2.0-blue)

More cool colored furniture and blocks! **Patch Up** adds 16 [Dye Depot](https://modrinth.com/mod/dye-depot) color variants to blocks, furniture, and devices from popular Minecraft mods.

---

## 📦 Requirements

* **Minecraft:** `1.21.1` (Fabric)
* **Java:** `21+`
* [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
* [Dye Depot](https://modrinth.com/mod/dye-depot) (`>= 2.0.0`)

---

## 📝 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use this mod in my modpack or make videos about it?**  
**A:** Yes! You are welcome to include Patch Up in your modpack or feature it in videos. Just please give credit and don't claim it as your own.

**Q: Will you backport to 1.16.5 or other older versions?**  
**A:** Backports are not planned. Currently, development focuses on `1.21.1` (with `1.20.1` being the earliest version ever supported).

**Q: Will there be a Forge, NeoForge, or Quilt version?**  
**A:** A NeoForge port is planned for the future. Quilt is currently undecided, and Forge is not planned.

**Q: Will you add compatibility for other mods?**  
**A:** Possibly! Feel free to leave a suggestion on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/EzfrxVqhEq) or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Ancient-Team/Patch-Up/issues).
